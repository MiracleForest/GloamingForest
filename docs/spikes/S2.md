# S2: Can the Twilight Forest generate its own chunks on Bedrock, deterministically and Java-exact, inside the custom dimension?

| | |
|---|---|
| Unit | F5 |
| Status | answered (custom biomes reach servers, clients and server maps through the chunk data) |
| Date | 2026-11-02 |
| Game / LeviLamina | 1.26.51 / 26.51.7 |

## Question

Can a mod-owned `WorldGenerator` produce every chunk of `twilightforest:twilight_forest` through a staged
pipeline (biomes, starts, noise, surface, blankets, carvers, features with structure pieces, spawns) on the
engine's generation worker threads, with the same random numbers and noise values as TF 4.8 on Java 1.21.1, and
can the chunks carry TF's custom biomes (server biomes, the client's view of them, spawn rules, map colours)?

## Why it matters

Everything world-shaped in TF depends on it: U1 (biome source, the 22 biomes), U2 (terrain, carvers, blankets),
U3 (trees), U18/U19 and every landmark unit (features, pieces, controlled spawns), U16/U17 (natural spawning),
U13 (maps), U15 (biome visuals). Parity of terrain and decoration needs bit-exact Java random sources and noises,
Java's seeding of every placed feature (`WorldgenRandom#setFeatureSeed(decorationSeed, featureIndex, step)` over
FeatureSorter's order), of every structure step and of every carver (`setLargeFeatureSeed(seed + index, sourceX,
sourceZ)`), Java's stage order and Java's biome lookups at block positions (BiomeManager's zoom).

## What was tried

1. **Java-parity core** (`src/core/jrand`, `src/core/noise`, pure C++): LegacyRandom, Xoroshiro128++
   (`RandomSupport` seeding, MD5 `fromHashOf`), WorldgenRandom (decoration, feature, large-feature, salted and
   slime seeds), positional factories, `Mth#getSeed`, String#hashCode, ImprovedNoise, SimplexNoise, PerlinNoise
   (modern and legacy), NormalNoise, PerlinSimplexNoise, the vanilla density functions with cubic splines,
   NoiseChunk interpolation, FeatureSorter, `HashSet<BlockPos>` order, `Mth#sin`, and **BiomeManager**
   (`src/core/jrand/BiomeManager.h`: `obfuscateSeed` = SHA-256 of the seed's little-endian bytes read back as
   Guava's `asLong`, `getFiddledDistance` with LinearCongruentialGenerator, the 8-corner zoom). Floating point
   code is compiled with `#pragma clang fp contract(off)`.
2. **Java oracle**: `tests/worldgen/oracle/Oracle.java` runs against the Minecraft 1.21.1 server jar (official
   mappings, reflection; JDK 21 and the jar from F13's `%LOCALAPPDATA%\gf-oracle`) and prints 107 lines (4 of them
   BiomeManager: `obfuscateSeed`, the quart `getBiome(BlockPos)` reads through a recording `NoiseBiomeSource`
   proxy, `getFiddledDistance`); `JavaParityTest` rebuilds every line and compares character for character.
3. **Generator**: `TwilightGenerator : VoidGenerator` (MCAPI constructor), installed by hooking
   `CustomDimension::$createGenerator` (MCAPI) for `twilightforest:twilight_forest`. `loadChunk` runs Biomes,
   Starts, Noise, Surface, **Blanket**, Carvers into the `LevelChunk`; `decorationPostProcessChunk` runs, for each
   `GenerationStep.Decoration` step, the **Pieces** stage then that step's placed features, then Features,
   LargeWriter and Spawns over a `BlockSource` of the 3x3 `ChunkViewSource` (Java's WorldGenRegion).
4. **Stage order as Java runs it** (`src/feature/ext/worldgen/Generation.h`):
   - Blanket at surface time: TF injects `ChunkBlanketProcessors#chunkBlanketing` right after
     `ChunkGenerator#buildSurface` in `ChunkStatusTasks#generateSurface` (tf-asm `ChunkStatusTaskTransformer`), so
     the canopy and the glacier are built over the bare ground (`WORLD_SURFACE_WG`) and every carver and feature
     sees them. `ext::blanketContext()` gives the blanket the surface step's WorldGenRegion random (each processor
     that applies forks it, as TF does) and the biomes of the chunk's sections. The contract header's stage
     comment ("Blanket" after "Pieces") is not Java's order.
   - Pieces per decoration step: `ext::decorationContext()` gives the step, the decoration seed and
     `structureRandom(index)` = `setFeatureSeed(decorationSeed, index, step)`, the seed Java gives the index-th
     structure of the step before `StructureStart#placeInChunk` (labyrinth / knight stronghold / troll cave at
     underground_structures, giant house at top_layer_modification after the vegetation).
   - Heights: `Chunk::height` is `ChunkAccess#getHeight` (the y of the highest counted block),
     `Region::height` is `WorldGenLevel#getHeight` (that y + 1).
5. **Biome lookups**: `Region::biome(pos)` is `LevelReader#getBiome(pos)`: BiomeManager's zoom over the quart
   biomes of the chunks the region holds (y clamped as `ChunkAccess#getNoiseBiome`), the biome source elsewhere
   (`getUncachedNoiseBiome`). The placement biome filter, freeze_top_layer, the chunk-generation and natural
   spawners and the blanket getter all go through it; `worldgen::biomeAt` zooms over the biome source. The
   generator's own writes stay raw quart biomes (`Chunk::setBiome`, `QuartBiomeWriter`): each engine biome is
   resolved once per chunk, a block column takes its most frequent biome in one `_setBiome(fill)` call and the
   other quarts per block.
6. **Carvers** as `ChunkGenerator#applyCarvers` (17 x 17 source chunks, `setLargeFeatureSeed(seed + k, S)`,
   `ext::carverContext()`); the chunk's carving mask is kept until its decoration, where
   `minecraft:carving_mask` (CarvingMaskPlacement) streams it in index order.
7. **Feature interpreter** (`src/feature/features`): imported configured / placed features, Java's lazy placement
   streams, 17 vanilla feature types, 20 placement modifiers (count_on_every_layer treats flowing water / lava as
   Java's water / lava; TF's `no_structure` skips invalid starts and pieces with `allow_features`), providers,
   predicates, rule tests, trees with placers and decorators, LargeWriter.
8. **Custom biomes**: the probe `gf_test:worldgen_probe` (behavior pack biome, format 1.21.110, `minecraft:climate`
   temperature 2.0 / downfall 0.0, `minecraft:map_tints` grass and foliage `#FF00FF`) is installed into the
   deployed test pack before the world loads (`tools/e2e/server/scenarios/worldgen.probe.ps1`), plus a client look
   in the test resource pack. Experiments, each with a vanilla-biome control:
   - painted chunks: `worldgen.custom_biome` paints 5 x 5 chunks 3000 blocks east of the real player with the probe
     and 5 x 5 chunks 3000 blocks south with `minecraft:desert` (same painting method), and keeps both loaded with
     a ticking area until `worldgen.custom_biome_restore`; the client has never received them; the player travels
     to each and `worldgen.custom_biome_view` logs what the server and the client's own level hold under it;
   - generated chunks (f4-dimension c9a4d02 merged locally, not pushed): `worldgen.generator_view` makes the
     generator write the probe into fresh Twilight Forest chunks (20000, 20000) and sends the player there, then
     the same with `minecraft:desert` at (40000, 40000);
   - server map: `worldgen.custom_biome_map` paints one chunk column with the probe and one with the desert next to
     an unpainted (plains) one, renders a server map over them with `MapItem::sampleMapData` (MCAPI) into a
     `MapItemSavedData` from `Level::createMapSavedData`, locates the texels with a lapis marker and logs the grass
     map colour of each column.
9. **Spawns**: tables, chunk-generation spawns (`spawnMobsForChunkGeneration` with the entity's heightmap,
   `adjustSpawnPosition` for ON_GROUND, the spawn box, the WorldGenRegion random for predicates, and the mob's
   spawn-position check before the group counts it), the natural spawner (`spawnCategoryForPosition` with the
   list re-checked at every position, the spawn box, the 24-block shared spawn exclusion, Java's global caps once
   per tick, `LocalMobCapCalculator`'s per-player caps, persistent mobs not counted, `getMaxSpawnClusterSize`,
   `isMaxGroupSizeReached`, `checkSpawnRules` / `checkSpawnObstruction` defaults with per-entity overrides through
   `spawn::ext::registerSpawnBehaviour`), TF's `gatherPotentialSpawns`, the structures' spawn overrides.
10. **Functions** `dev_new_world` / `dev_powerwash_chunk` as Bedrock functions; dev_new_world's TF half through
    `scriptevent twilightforest:dev_new_world` (hook on the MCAPI `ScriptEventCommand::$execute`).

  answers (FIPS 180-4).
- Selftests `worldgen.*` (24, server and client builds; `tools/e2e/server/run.ps1 -Scenario worldgen` exit 0,

## Conclusion

Yes. The CustomDimension route gives a mod generator for the custom dimension; loadChunk and decoration
post-processing give the two halves of Java's ChunkStatus pipeline, the stages run in Java's order (blankets at
surface time, structures at their own decoration step with Java's seeds), the core reproduces Java's numbers bit for
bit, and block-position biome lookups use Java's BiomeManager zoom.

Custom biomes work end to end through the chunk data:
- Server: a behavior pack biome with a namespaced id registers on BDS 1.26.51 without an experiment; the generator
  writes it; features, spawns, temperature and the biome source see it.
- Clients: the chunk's biome reaches the client's own level (the client mod is not involved in chunk
  deserialization, so this is the same for vanilla clients) and the client draws the biome's climate-based look.
  TF's biome visuals therefore come from U1's biome definitions and U15's resource pack client biome files; F12 /
  U15 add only what Bedrock biome files cannot express (TF's sky and fog renderers in `client.world`).
- Map colours: server maps colour grass and foliage by the chunk's biome, including a custom biome's
  `minecraft:map_tints`, so U1 gives each TF biome its map tints; TF's magic / maze maps use `magic_map_color` (U13).
- Spawn rules: F5's spawner spawns the biomes' mobs from the biome JSON spawners (no vanilla spawn-rule tags on TF
  biomes, so Bedrock's own spawner adds nothing).

Limits: providers run on worker threads and touch only their chunk / region. Bedrock vines have no "up" face and
leaves no distance property; Bedrock grass has no "snowy" state. Mob size, cluster size and spawn checks default to
Java's base classes until the entity units register theirs (`spawn::ext::registerSpawnBehaviour`); generation-time
light for spawn checks is estimated from the blocks above (Bedrock lights chunks later). The probe's resource pack
look in the legacy `biomes_client.json` (magenta fog / water) did not apply on 1.26.51; U15 should author TF's
client biomes in the current client biome format.

## Fallback and follow-ups

- If a game update breaks the hook, F4 can construct the generator through `ext::createGenerator`.
- U1: `gf::worldgen::ext::setPossibleBiomes` with the biome source's order; the 22 biomes as behavior pack biomes
  with climate and `minecraft:map_tints`; no vanilla spawn-rule tags.
- U2: terrain on `core::noise::NoiseChunk`; carvers through `ext::carverContext()`; blankets are called at surface
  time with `ext::blanketContext()` (fork its `regionRandom` per processor that applies, read `chunkBiomes`).
- F6 / structure units: a Pieces step is called once per decoration step: place only the structures of
  `decorationContext()->step`, each with `structureRandom(index)` (index = its position among the step's structures
  in Java's registry order). Piece spawn indices through `spawn::ext::registerSpawnIndex` or `spawn_indices`;
  `allow_features` (one bool per piece box) for TF's UtilityPiece. Controlled spawns and `no_structure` read starts
  through `landmark::startsIntersecting` (F6; re-verify once F6 lands).
- Entity units: `spawn::ext::registerSpawnBehaviour` for size, cluster size and spawn checks (TF's ghasts, golems,
  kobolds, penguins, giant miners, ...), `setPlacementHeightmap` where an entity registers another heightmap.
