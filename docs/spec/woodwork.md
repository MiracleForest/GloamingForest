# Wood sets and wooden furniture (U4 `woodwork`)

Behaviour spec of unit **U4** (wave W1), translated from the upstream 4.8 (`9e40a757`, MC 1.21.1) by F16. It is
the checklist the U4 worker implements and the A2 audit compares against. Section 1-5 are the behaviour; Appendix A lists
every id U4 owns with upstream's registration, block states, loot, recipes, tags and sounds; Appendix B the strings.

## 1. Scope

U4 owns the wooden building set of the eight upstream woods and the wooden furniture of every wood:

- **Eight upstream wood sets** (`gloaming_oak`, `canopy`, `mangrove`, `dark`, `time`, `transformation`, `mining`, `sorting`):
  log, wood, stripped log, stripped wood, planks, stairs, slab, button, fence, fence gate, pressure plate, door,
  trapdoor, sign + wall sign, hanging sign + wall hanging sign, chest, trapped chest, boat, chest boat.
- **Banisters** and **drying racks** for all 19 woods (8 upstream + oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, crimson,
  warped, "vangrove" = vanilla mangrove, bamboo, cherry).
- **Hollow logs** (horizontal, vertical, climbable) for 18 woods (8 upstream + 10 vanilla; no bamboo) and their 18 items.
- `canopy_bookshelf`, the drying recipe type `gloamingforest:drying` (20 upstream recipes: kelp, mud, sapling, sponge, tanned
  leather, 2 gelatinous drops, stale bread, 12 jerkies; + 15 vanilla coral recipes), the `drying_rack` block entity,
  particle and two sounds, and the leather / slime items made on racks or from them (`tannin`, `treated_leather`,
  `tanned_leather`, `gelatinous_slime_drop`, `gelatinous_maze_slime_drop`).
- 230 recipes (wood set crafting, drying) with their recipe-unlock advancements, 245 block loot tables, 25 tags.

Not U4 (string references only, R2): leaves, saplings, potted saplings and magic log cores (U3 `trees`); the
`chiseled_canopy_bookshelf` (U22); giant logs (U29); `mangrove_root` (U3); the camp drying-rack loot
(`camp_drying_rack`, U19) and the structure marker that fills racks (F7/U19); `item/#gloamingforest:logs` and
`#gloamingforest:lower_on_drying_rack` (F9 tags, read here).

Contracts (units.json): families+blockevents, inventory, recipes, entity, client.render, interactions.

### 3.2 Banister (`BanisterBlock`, 19 blocks)

- Properties: `ofFullCopy(<wood>_planks)` (vanilla woods copy the vanilla planks; `vangrove_banister` copies
  `minecraft:mangrove_planks`).
- States: `facing` (horizontal), `shape` = `short` | `tall` | `connected` (enum order SHORT, TALL, CONNECTED; default
  `tall`), `extended` (bool, default false), `waterlogged`.
- Placement: `facing` = the player's horizontal facing; `shape` = `connected` when the block above is in
  `#gloamingforest:banisters`, else `tall`; waterlogged in water.
- Axe right-click (any item with the `axe_wax_off` ability, i.e. any axe): plays `item.axe.strip` (volume 1, pitch 1),
  then `shape` cycles to the next value (short -> tall -> connected -> short). If the block below is a banister whose
  shape is not `short`, `extended` is forced false; otherwise, when the new shape is `tall` (a full cycle), `extended`
  also toggles. Always succeeds (consumes the click, no durability loss).
- Neighbour update: when a non-short banister appears directly below an extended banister, the upper one un-extends.
- Item tooltip: `block.gloamingforest.banister.cycle` ("Right-click with axe to cycle"), gray.
- Pathfinding: treated as a fence (`PathType.FENCE`).
- Outline / collision shapes (pixels; north facing, others rotated): rail `tall` = box(0,12,0, 16,16,4) + two posts
  box(2.5,0,0, 5.5,16,3) and box(10.5,0,0, 13.5,16,3); `short` = rail box(0,4,0, 16,8,4) + posts to y 4; `connected` =
  the two 16-high posts only; `extended` adds the posts continued down to y -8 (box(2.5,-8,0, 5.5,0,3),
  box(10.5,-8,0, 13.5,0,3)).
- Fuel: item tag `#gloamingforest:banisters` burns **300 ticks** (`neoforge:furnace_fuels`).

### 3.3 Drying rack (`DryingRackBlock`, `DryingRackBlockEntity`, `DryingRecipe`; 19 blocks)

- Properties: `copyAndScaleProperties(<wood>_slab, 0.5)` = the slab's properties with destroy time and explosion
  resistance halved (wooden slab 2.0/3.0 -> **1.0 / 1.5**).
- States: `facing` (horizontal; the player's facing at placement), `waterlogged`. Shape: a 16x4x4 bar at the top of
  the block against the `facing` side (north: box(0,12,0, 16,16,4); south box(0,12,12, 16,16,16); west
  box(0,12,0, 4,16,16); east box(12,12,0, 16,16,16)). Render shape MODEL. Rotates / mirrors with structures.
- Use with an item on an **empty** rack: puts **one** item (creative players keep theirs), sound
  `block.gloamingforest.drying_rack.add_item` (volume 1.0, pitch 0.75 + rand*0.5), game event `block_change`.
- Use on a rack holding an item (any hand content): the item goes to the inventory or is dropped at the player;
  sound `...remove_item` (volume 0.75, pitch 0.75 + rand*0.5), game event `block_change`. Empty rack with empty hand:
  no action (pass to the item's own use).
- Drying (server tick, only while **not waterlogged**): if the held item matches a `gloamingforest:drying` recipe,
  `dryTime` counts up every tick; at `dryTime >= totalDryTime` the item is replaced by the recipe result (count from
  the recipe, stack limited to 1) and the block is synced. `totalDryTime` = the recipe's `filter_time`, default
  `DEFAULT_DRYING_TIME` = 20*60*5 = **6000** ticks when no recipe. Changing the item (different item/components)
  resets `dryTime` to 0 and recomputes. `drying` = recipe present and not waterlogged; a change is synced to clients
  (update tag `drying`, `item`). Saved NBT: `item`, `dry_time`, `total_dry_time`.
- Client particles: while `drying`, each animate tick with chance 1/5, one `gloamingforest:drying_rack` particle on the
  `facing` face (`ParticleUtils.spawnParticleOnFace(level, pos, facing, type, Vec3.ZERO, 0.3)`). The particle is a
  `BaseAshSmokeParticle` (quad size x0.5, colour multiplier 0.3, lifetime 16, gravity -0.025, physics on) tinted
  RGB (0.7294118, 0.69411767, 0.7607843).
- Any state change of the rack drops the held item (`DryingRackBlock.onRemove` has no same-block check, and the
  server calls it on every state change): breaking or replacing it, **becoming waterlogged** (a water source placed
  into it, e.g. a water bucket; flowing water never waterlogs it, `SimpleWaterloggedBlock` accepts only source water)
  or losing its water (bucket pickup), and any other in-world state change of the same rack (vanilla has none for
  `facing`: `rotate` / `mirror` only shape the state a structure template places, which is a new block, not a change).
  The item is ejected with `Containers.dropItemStack` at the block position and the rack stays, now empty, when only
  its state changed. On Bedrock, a water source entering or leaving the rack's liquid layer counts as the waterlogged
  change; flowing water does not.
- Automation (item handler, any side): 1 slot, limit 1; insert only when empty (one item); extract is refused while
  `drying`.
- Renderer (`DryingRackRenderer`): the item is drawn with display context FIXED at x = 0.5 + 0.4*facing.stepX,
  z = 0.5 + 0.4*facing.stepZ, y = 0.5 for 3D block models, 0.325 for items in `#gloamingforest:lower_on_drying_rack`,
  else 0.45; banners 0.4 lower, shields 0.1 lower; scale 0.99; rotated by -facing.yRot about Y.
- Loot fill: `fillFromLootTable(table, seed)` sets the held item from a chest-type loot roll (used by camp
  structures through the structure marker handler; U19 owns the table, U4 provides the fill hook).
- Drying recipes (`gloamingforest:drying`, JSON `{input, result, filter_time}`; not shown in the recipe book,
  `isSpecial`), 20 upstream recipes plus 15 coral recipes: `drying/kelp` (kelp -> dried kelp, 3600 t),
  `drying/mud_to_clay` (mud -> clay, 7200 t), `drying/sapling_to_dead_bush` (`#minecraft:saplings` -> dead bush,
  7200 t), `drying/sponge` (wet sponge -> sponge, 2400 t), `tanned_leather` (treated leather, 6000 t),
  `gelatinous_slime_drop` (slime ball, 6000 t), `gelatinous_maze_slime_drop` (`gloamingforest:maze_slime_ball`,
  6000 t), `stale_bread` (bread -> stale bread, 6000 t) and the 12 jerkies `<x>_jerky` (6000 t each): beef,
  chicken, cod, fugu (pufferfish), meef (raw meef), monster (rotten flesh), mutton, pork (porkchop), rabbit, salmon,
  tropical_fish, venison (raw venison) -> the U10 jerky item; plus the 15 `minecraft:dead_*_coral*` recipes (live
  coral, coral block or fan -> its dead form in 40 t). Inputs, results and times of every one are in the Recipes
  table of Appendix A (all owned by U4, R4).

### 3.4 Hollow logs (54 blocks, 18 items)

Each wood has three blocks sharing one item `hollow_<w>_log` (`HollowLogItem`, burn time **300**): the item places
the **vertical** block when the clicked face is up/down and the **horizontal** block when it is a side face; the
climbable block has no item of its own (picks the hollow log item).

- `hollow_<w>_log_horizontal` (`HorizontalHollowLogBlock`, bark properties; vanilla woods copy `<w>_wood` /
  `*_hyphae`): states `horizontal_axis` (x/z, from the clicked face's axis) and `variant` = `empty` | `moss` |
  `moss_and_grass` | `snow` | `waterlogged` (waterlogged when placed in water). Shape: full block minus a 12x12 tunnel
  along the axis (2..14); with moss / grass / snow the floor rises to y 3; collision: minus a 14x14 tunnel (1..15),
  the carpet is not collidable. Clicks count only inside the tunnel (hit 0.124..0.876 on both cross axes):
  - moss patch (`gloamingforest:moss_patch`) on `empty` (or `waterlogged` when neither tunnel end touches water) ->
    `moss`, place sound of the moss patch, consumes one;
  - short grass on `moss` -> `moss_and_grass`;
  - snowball on `empty` (same water rule) -> `snow`, snow place sound;
  - shovel on `snow` -> `empty`, snow break sound, drops a snowball (non-creative, shovel loses 1 durability);
  - shears on `moss`/`moss_and_grass` -> `empty`, sheep shear sound, drops the moss patch (+ short grass);
  - empty hand while sneaking, facing along the axis, at the same y and within 0.81 of the bottom centre: the player
    switches to the **swimming (crawling) pose**.
  Place / break sounds use the source block's sound with volume (v+1)/2, pitch p*0.8.
- `hollow_<w>_log_vertical` (`VerticalHollowLogBlock`, stripped properties): `waterlogged`; shape full block minus
  12x12 shaft (collision minus 14x14). Vine on the inside -> climbable `variant=vine`; ladder -> `ladder` (or
  `ladder_waterlogged` if waterlogged); `facing` = the clicked face if horizontal, else opposite of the player's facing.
- `hollow_<w>_log_climbable` (`ClimbableHollowLogBlock`): `facing`, `variant` = `vine` | `ladder` |
  `ladder_waterlogged`; in `#minecraft:climbable` (via `#gloamingforest:hollow_logs_climbable`). Ladder variants add a
  3-px ladder plate on the facing side to shape and collision. Shears inside -> back to the vertical log (keeps water
  for `ladder_waterlogged`), sheep shear sound, drops the vine or ladder, shears lose 1. Waterlogging a vine variant
  turns it into a waterlogged vertical log; ladder <-> ladder_waterlogged.
- Loot (all three): Silk Touch -> the hollow log item, otherwise **2-4 sticks** (+ Fortune uniform bonus 1); plus the
  content: moss/moss_and_grass/snow/vine only with Silk Touch (moss patch, short grass, snowball, vine), ladders
  always drop the ladder.
- Vanilla-wood hollow logs: `hollow_vangrove_log_vertical` and `hollow_vangrove_log_climbable` use explicit
  properties (`ignitedByLava`, COLOR_RED, `strength(2.0)`, WOOD) instead of copying stripped mangrove wood;
  `hollow_vangrove_log_horizontal` copies `Blocks.MANGROVE_WOOD` (`ofFullCopy`).

### 3.5 Leather, tannin and slime drops

`tannin` (shapeless: water bottle + gloaming oak sapling + root strand + gloaming oak leaves; craft remainder: glass
bottle), `treated_leather` (shapeless: tannin + `#c:leathers`) and `tanned_leather` (treated leather dried 6000 t) are
plain items used by U11's travellers' gear (by string). `gelatinous_slime_drop` (food: nutrition 1, saturation 0.2,
Speed for 600 t, chance 1.0) and `gelatinous_maze_slime_drop` (nutrition 2, saturation 0.3, Resistance 600 t) are
dried slime balls; both render lower on the drying rack.

## 4. Bedrock adaptation notes

- Wood set members are **family** entries (`assets/data/families/woodwork.json`: `log`, `wood`, `pillar`, `stairs`,
  `slab`, `button`, `fence`, `fence_gate`, `pressure_plate`, `door`, `trapdoor`, `sign`, `hanging_sign`, `chest`,
  `trapped_chest`), so F11 generates the BP JSON and the generic behaviours (stripping through the `log` family's
  stripped target, door halves, slab doubling). Keep upstream's hardness / resistance (Appendix A) in the family params.
- Chests, trapped chests: custom blocks have no block entities (S3); declare them as containers
  (`assets/data/containers/woodwork.json`, 27 slots) and use the families `chest` / `trapped_chest` pairing; the
  trapped chest's redstone output goes through `redstone::registerEmitter`. Textures from the imported chest atlas.
- Signs and hanging signs: `gf::signs` (F11) keeps text; the client mod renders upstream's sign textures.
- Drying rack: held item and timers in **BlockData** (`gf::blockdata`, keys under the block) or the S3 backend via
  `gf::inventory::registerBlock` (1 slot, insert/extract rules above); tick through `blockevents` + a per-rack
  scheduler (racks must dry while loaded, not only on random ticks: keep a loaded-rack set). The item is drawn by
  `client::render::registerBlockRenderer` (client mod); vanilla clients see the empty rack (best effort: an item
  display entity may be added if S3 allows it). Recipes load through `gf::recipes::registerType("gloamingforest:drying")`
  from the imported JSON.
- Banister axe cycling and hollow-log interactions: `interactions::onUseOn({.block = ...})`, return `Consume`;
  block-state edits from a cancelled interaction go to `interactions::nextTick` (AGENTS.md). Hit-position checks
  use the click position Bedrock reports.
- Climbable hollow logs are climbable **only with the client mod** (deviation in README); vanilla clients get no
  climbing (not-ported.md). The crawling pose in horizontal hollow logs is approximated on vanilla clients (Bedrock has
  crawling under 1-block gaps natively; trigger it with the sneak-click rule via `movement`).
- Boats: Bedrock has no custom boat types; upstream boats are custom entities (U4-owned BP entities with boat physics from
  `minecraft:boat` runtime behaviour) or an `entity` contract registration; the items place them. The chest boat needs
  a container (inventory contract). Document the chosen route in `docs/units/woodwork.md`.
- Fire spread (5/5, 5/20) maps to `minecraft:flammable` components (catch_chance_modifier / destroy_chance_modifier);
  fuel 300 t to the item's `minecraft:fuel` (15 s).
- Jar lids are U6's concern; U4 only keeps the log ids stable.

## 5. Acceptance checklist (selftests `woodwork.*`, dx 400-499)

- Every block in Appendix A exists with upstream's hardness, resistance, sound, map colour and states; loot tables match
  (doors drop from the lower half only, bookshelf 2 books without Silk Touch, hollow logs 2-4 sticks).
- Stripping each upstream log and wood with an axe gives the stripped block with the same axis.
- Banister: placement shape rule, axe cycle order short -> tall -> connected with `extended` toggling on the full cycle,
  forced un-extend over a non-short banister.
- Drying rack: add/remove sounds, dried kelp after 3600 ticks, coral after 40 ticks, no drying while waterlogged,
  default 6000 when no recipe, item dropped on break, item also dropped (rack stays, empty) when the rack becomes
  waterlogged by a water bucket or loses its water (flowing water does not waterlog it), hopper cannot extract while
  drying.
- Hollow logs: item places vertical on top faces and horizontal on side faces; moss / grass / snow / vine / ladder /
  shears / shovel transitions and drops; climbable variant is in `#minecraft:climbable`.
- Chest pairing and trapped chest redstone; sign editing; boats placeable and rideable.
- Recipes: all 230 recipe ids load and craft the listed results; their unlock advancements fire on the `has_*` items.
